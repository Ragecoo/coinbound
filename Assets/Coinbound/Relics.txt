Artifacts and the coin chest
============================
Original art/names: ура/items/DESC.txt. Current tuning and the full before/after changelog:
BALANCE_CHANGES.txt in the project root (also copied beside the Windows executable).
32 relics: positive tiers 12 / 7 / 2, negative tiers 6 / 3 / 2.
Damage, speed and attack rate modifiers are additive fractions of the hero's base stats.
Enemy aura modifiers are percentages; all auras remain invisible, radius 5 tiles.
HP, defense and luck are flat additions. Critical chance additions are percentage points.
Inventory bounds prevent impossible runs; exact values and probability formulas are in the changelog.
The coin remains fair (50/50), with luck affecting rarity only. No reroll on closing.
Duplicates stack and each occurrence has its own 48x48 HUD icon, four per row.

Camera
------
After the cards finish revealing, the camera starts automatically. The tracker process
starts on a worker thread. Frames remain in memory and use a local anonymous pipe.
Only the blue pixel hand skeleton is displayed; no video recording.
Sequence (restored 2026-11-09): fist for 0.30 seconds, then thumbs-up for 0.12 seconds,
within 5 seconds. At least three fingers curled; small thumb extension is accepted.
Tracking gaps up to 0.8 seconds are tolerated. No wrist lift is required.
A human camera sequence still needs live validation; automated tests use synthetic landmarks.
F2 spawns a nearby debug chest; F3 opens the paged relic grant menu in development/editor builds.
