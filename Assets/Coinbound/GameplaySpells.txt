VOICE SPELLS

Pulse: 20 mana, 3 s cooldown. Nearest partly visible enemy within 8 tiles.
Projectile deals 2x base damage in a 2-tile splash; stone blocks splash damage.
An empty cast spends mana and cooldown but creates no projectile.
Trace: 15 mana, 6 s cooldown, 3 s duration. Red floor path to nearest enemy,
blue path to the stairs. Uses skull/stairs icons supplied in ura/icons.
Ward: 40 mana, 10 s cooldown, 3 s immunity. Gold pixel shield;
nearby enemies are repelled about 1.5 tiles on a shield impact.
Decoy: 25 mana, 12 s cooldown, 5 s duration. Frozen white translucent hero pose;
enemies within 8 tiles pursue the decoy, then return to the player.
All spells can be spent without targets. There is no automatic mana regeneration.

Recognition: local Vosk 0.3.45, English and Russian small models, Windows x64.
Commands: pulse / trace / ward / decoy and their spoken Russian equivalents.
Two models run on a worker thread. Microphone capture uses 16 kHz mono, 30 ms blocks.
Utterance end silence tuned to 0.20 / 0.35 / 0.60 s, minimum word confidence 0.90.
Unknown speech is allowed in the grammar and ignored; duplicates are suppressed.
Mic stops in menus, pause, death, loss of focus, and automated batch-mode checks.
Settings include a voice toggle; HUD displays loading, listening or microphone errors.
Audio is neither saved nor sent to servers. Recognition is not guaranteed in noise.
Models and native dependencies are bundled with the build; no language-pack install.

Developer checks:
Coinbound.Tests.DungeonSpellTests covers mana, cooldowns, walls, AOE, ward and decoy.
Development build: -batchmode -coinbound-spell-capture <output-folder>
produces deterministic screenshots without opening the microphone.

F1 voice debug: separate bounded text box with live EN/RU hypotheses and finished phrases.
Uses full-vocabulary decoders only while visible; command words are green.
Old text is removed when the box fills. No transcripts are saved to disk.
